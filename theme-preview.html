3. Planned Webpage Architecture & Sections

The single common webpage for SSRR Lab will feature the following distinct sections:

1. What is this (About SSRR Lab)

Overview of the lab, institutional affiliation, identity, and academic mission.

2. What does it do

Research focus areas, geospatial intelligence, algorithm design, and spatial modeling.

3. Software Motivation & Gist

Why we build open source: reproducibility, global accessibility, and real-world deployment.

4. What we have created

Showcase of the 3 open source packages with GitHub links and standalone page placeholders.

5. Funding Agency

Acknowledgment of sponsoring research councils, grant bodies, and collaborating partners.

6. Where is this (Location)

Department, university/institute address, physical location, and contact information.
